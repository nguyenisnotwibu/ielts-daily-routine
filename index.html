3 điểm ngữ pháp khi mô tả thói quen trong IELTS.

LÝ THUYẾT

Adverbs of Frequency — Trạng từ tần suất

Nói bạn làm gì thường xuyên — quan trọng trong Speaking Part 1.

✅ Thứ tự
always (100%) → usually (80%) → often (60%) → sometimes (40%) → rarely (20%) → never (0%)
📌 Vị trí
S + adverb + V → I usually wake up at 6.
S + BE + adverb → She is always on time.
✓ I usually have breakfast before school.
✓ I sometimes study at the library.
✗ I have usually breakfast. (sai vị trí)
💡 Adverbs of frequency giúp câu trả lời Part 1 tự nhiên hơn.
LÝ THUYẾT

Prepositions of Time — AT / IN / ON

Lỗi rất phổ biến ở band 4–5.

✅ Quy tắc
AT + giờ: at 7 AM, at noon, at night
IN + buổi/tháng/năm: in the morning, in June
ON + ngày/thứ: on Monday, on weekends
✓ I wake up at 6:30.
✓ In the afternoon, I play football.
✓ On Saturdays, I visit my grandparents.
✗ I wake up in 6:30. (giờ → at)
💡 Nói đúng at/in/on = dấu hiệu band 6+.
LÝ THUYẾT

Sequence Words — Từ nối trình tự

Mô tả thói quen theo thứ tự — cần trong Speaking & Writing.

✅ Các từ nối
First → Then / Next → After that → Finally
Before + V-ing • After + V-ing
✓ First, I brush my teeth. Then, I have breakfast.
✓ After finishing homework, I watch TV.
✓ Before going to bed, I read a book.
💡 Sequence words tăng điểm Coherence & Cohesion.
